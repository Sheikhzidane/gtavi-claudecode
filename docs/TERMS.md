# GTA Viral Clips Publisher: Terms of Service

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. **What it is.** GTA Viral Clips Publisher is a private publishing tool run by the owner of the GTA Viral Clips accounts. It is used only with the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts. It is not offered to other users.
2. **What it does.** The tool uploads short videos with titles and captions that the owner has prepared, through each platform's official API. Before a video is published, the tool checks its format and length, its captions and creator credit, and that its rights are recorded as cleared.
3. **Content rights.** The tool only publishes footage that the owner made, or footage recorded as authorised for clipping by its creator (for example through a clipping campaign the owner joined, or written permission). Creators are credited in each caption. If a platform reports a copyright or account problem, the tool stops all publishing automatically until the owner reviews it. Rights questions or takedown requests: open an issue at the address below.
4. **Platform rules.** The tool uses the platforms' official APIs within their terms, rate limits and community guidelines.
5. **No warranty.** The tool is provided "as is", without warranties of any kind.
6. **Not affiliated.** GTA Viral Clips is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Rockstar Games, Take-Two Interactive, Google, TikTok or Meta.
